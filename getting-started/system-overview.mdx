Coaching OS is designed to give you a single, organized home for everything in your coaching practice — your methodology, your business, and your clients — in a structure that evolves alongside you. Rather than scattering your thinking across notes apps, spreadsheets, and memory, this system brings it together so you can show up fully in every session and make better decisions about your practice over time.

## How the Pillars Connect

The three pillars aren't independent silos — they form a continuous loop that strengthens your practice over time.

Your **Coaching Craft** informs how you show up in every session. The frameworks you've documented, the philosophy you've articulated, and the session structures you've refined all shape the quality of your coaching presence. When your craft is explicit and well-documented, you bring more intention to every conversation.

Your **Business Operations** govern how clients move through your practice — from first contact to final session. Consistent processes mean clients get a reliable experience, and you spend less energy reinventing logistics on the fly.

Your **Client Knowledge Bases** capture what actually happens when craft meets a real person in a real coaching relationship. The notes, patterns, and progress you record feed back into your understanding of what works — and that learning sharpens your craft and improves your operations.

Together, these three pillars create a learning loop: you apply your craft, your business supports the engagement, and your client records capture the outcomes. Over time, your system becomes a compounding asset — one that makes you a better coach with every client you serve.

## Keeping the System Alive

A coaching system that doesn't get updated stops being useful quickly. The most important habit you can build is returning to Coaching OS regularly — not just to add new information, but to review what's already there, notice what's shifted, and make small updates that keep the system accurate.

This doesn't have to be a major undertaking. A few minutes at the end of a session to log a note, or fifteen minutes at the end of the week to review your frameworks, is enough to keep the momentum going.

<Note>
  Build a regular review rhythm into your practice. A brief weekly check-in to update session notes and flag follow-ups, combined with a monthly review of your frameworks, client progress, and business metrics, is enough to keep Coaching OS current and genuinely useful. The Growth & Reflection section of the system is designed to support exactly this kind of structured reflection.
</Note>

## Recommended Starting Order

If you're setting up Coaching OS for the first time, this sequence will help you build a solid foundation before adding complexity.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with Coaching Craft">
    Document your coaching philosophy and at least one core framework. Making your approach explicit — even in rough form — gives the rest of your system a foundation to build on. Visit **Coaching Craft → Coaching Philosophy** and **Frameworks** to get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Document Your Business Operations">
    Capture your client lifecycle stages, your current packages, and any recurring workflows you already use. Even if your processes are informal right now, writing them down creates consistency and makes it easier to improve them. Visit **Business Operations** to explore these sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a Client Knowledge Base for Each Active Client">
    Create a profile for every client you're currently working with. Start with their goals, intake context, and any agreements you've made. Then begin logging session notes after each meeting. Visit **Client Knowledge Bases → Client Profiles** to get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Growth & Reflection to Continuously Improve">
    Once the foundational sections are in place, use the Growth & Reflection pillar to establish a review practice. Capture insights from your work, reflect on your development as a coach, and revisit your frameworks as your thinking evolves. Visit **Growth & Reflection → Reviewing Your Practice** to begin.
  </Step>
</Steps>
