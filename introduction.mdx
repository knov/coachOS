Coaching OS is your personal operating system for building and running a coaching practice. It brings together the three pillars of a thriving coaching business — your **craft**, your **operations**, and your **clients** — into one coherent, evolving system that grows with you.

Whether you're refining a coaching framework, onboarding a new client, or reviewing how a session went, Coaching OS gives you a dedicated home for every piece of your practice so nothing slips through the cracks.

## What Coaching OS Gives You

Coaching OS is organized around three core areas:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Coaching Craft" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Capture your philosophy, evolve your frameworks, and document how you coach — so your approach becomes explicit, teachable, and improvable.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Business Operations" icon="briefcase">
    Run your business with clarity. Manage the client lifecycle, define your packages, and keep your workflows consistent and efficient.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Client Knowledge Bases" icon="folder-open">
    Every client gets their own compartmentalized space — profiles, session notes, and progress tracking — completely separate from every other client.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to Navigate This System

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with the Quick Start">
    Orient yourself with the [Quick Start](/getting-started/quick-start) to understand how the system is laid out and where to begin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Document Your Craft">
    Head to [Coaching Craft](/coaching-craft/coaching-philosophy) to begin capturing your philosophy, frameworks, and session structure. This is the foundation everything else builds on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Business Operations">
    Visit [Business Operations](/business/business-overview) to document your client lifecycle, packages, and workflows so your business runs predictably.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your First Client">
    Open [Client Knowledge Bases](/clients/overview) and create a profile, log your first session notes, and start tracking progress.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  This system is designed to evolve. Start with what feels most urgent — your craft, a specific client, or a business process — and build from there. There's no wrong place to begin.
</Tip>
