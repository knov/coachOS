Every client follows a journey through your business. Mapping this lifecycle helps you create a consistent, professional experience and ensures nothing falls through the cracks at any stage — whether that's a missed follow-up after a discovery call or an offboarding conversation that never happens.

## The Five Stages

Each client moves through five distinct stages in your practice. Understanding these stages lets you design the right experience, have the right assets ready, and give every client the attention they deserve at each point in the relationship.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discovery">
    This is how potential clients find you and make first contact. It typically includes an initial inquiry — through your website, a referral, or social media — followed by a discovery call where you both explore fit, goals, and what working together could look like. Your job at this stage is to create a clear, welcoming entry point and help the prospective client understand whether coaching with you is the right move.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enrollment">
    Once there's a mutual yes, the enrollment stage turns interest into a formal engagement. This covers sending a proposal or offer, signing the coaching agreement, processing payment, and delivering onboarding materials. A smooth enrollment builds trust immediately and sets the tone for the entire relationship.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Active Engagement">
    This is the core of the coaching relationship. It includes regular sessions, between-session check-ins, and milestone reviews that track progress toward the client's goals. Your focus here is fully on the coaching work — helping the client move, reflect, and grow. Everything else in your system exists to support this stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Closure">
    The closure stage brings the engagement to a deliberate, meaningful end. It includes a final session designed for reflection and integration, a formal offboarding process, and — when the time is right — asking for a testimonial or referral. A thoughtful closure honors the work done and leaves the relationship in good standing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Alumni">
    Former clients don't disappear — they become part of your alumni network. Staying in touch, sharing relevant content, and making re-engagement offers when appropriate keeps the relationship alive and turns satisfied clients into advocates who refer others to your practice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to Have Ready at Each Stage

Knowing the stages is only half the picture. The other half is having the right assets and actions prepared before you need them. Use the accordions below to review what belongs at each stage of the lifecycle.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Discovery">
    - **Intake form** — a short form that captures the prospective client's situation, goals, and how they found you
    - **Discovery call agenda** — a consistent structure for your exploratory conversations so you cover what matters
    - **FAQs** — answers to the questions prospects ask most often, ready to share or reference
    - **Pricing one-pager** — a clear, concise overview of your packages and investment levels
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Enrollment">
    - **Proposal template** — a customizable document that outlines the engagement scope, outcomes, and investment
    - **Coaching agreement** — your standard contract covering scope, confidentiality, cancellation, and expectations
    - **Payment instructions** — clear details on how and when payment is processed
    - **Welcome packet** — an onboarding document that orients the new client to how you work together
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Active Engagement">
    - **Session note template** — a consistent format for capturing what happened, what emerged, and what's next
    - **Progress check-in cadence** — a scheduled touchpoint (often at the midpoint) to assess how the engagement is going
    - **Assessments** — any tools, frameworks, or worksheets you use at the midpoint to measure growth or shift direction
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Closure">
    - **Final session agenda** — a structure for closing the engagement with intention and reflection
    - **Reflection worksheet** — a document that helps the client articulate their growth, wins, and next steps
    - **Testimonial request** — a simple, friendly ask for a written or video testimonial
    - **Referral ask** — a natural invitation to introduce others who might benefit from working with you
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Alumni">
    - **Periodic check-in schedule** — a plan for staying in touch at 30, 60, or 90 days post-engagement
    - **Newsletter or content list** — resources, insights, or updates you share with alumni to stay top of mind
    - **Re-engagement offer** — a clearly defined path for alumni who are ready to work together again
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
