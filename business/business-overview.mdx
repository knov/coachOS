## Why Document Your Operations

When your operations live in your head, every client engagement requires you to reinvent the wheel. When they're documented, you gain something much more valuable: consistency. Documented operations let you deliver a predictable, professional experience to every client, onboard new clients with confidence, delegate or automate admin tasks, and quickly identify where things break down or create friction.

<Tip>
  You don't need to document everything at once. Start with the processes you repeat most often — onboarding, session scheduling, and closing engagements. Build from there.
</Tip>

## The Operating Rhythm

A well-run practice isn't just a collection of documented processes — it runs on a rhythm. Think of your business in three cadences:

- **Weekly** — deliver client sessions, handle scheduling and communication, log session notes while they're fresh
- **Monthly** — review your active client roster, check revenue and capacity, update workflows that caused friction
- **Quarterly** — step back and assess your market positioning, packages, pricing, and the direction you want to grow

Each cadence keeps a different part of your business healthy. The weekly work sustains your clients. The monthly review keeps your operations sharp. The quarterly check-in keeps your business aligned with where you want to go.
